---------------------------------------------------------------
NOTES
---------------------------------------------------------------
* Handle corrected to @amanjharjhariya.

* Instagram shows about two lines before "more". Put the strongest
  line first - that is why each option opens on a lyric, not on
  the credits.

* Put the YouTube link in your bio, not the caption. Links in
  captions are not clickable and Instagram tends to show that
  post to fewer people.

* Use Reels audio as the original audio so others can use your
  sound. That is free distribution.

* 8-10 hashtags beats 30. The broad ones (#music #trending) put
  you against millions of posts; the narrow ones (#desihiphop
  #hindirap) are where people actually find a new artist.
